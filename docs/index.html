Tunnl

A study monitor that keeps you focused, tracks your progress, and tells you what to study next.

See it in action

Short clips, no sound.

Stay on task without a lecture

Tunnl watches what you're doing while a session runs, and speaks up only when you drift.

🧭

Knows where you are

Detects the app in front and the active tab in Chrome, Safari, Brave, Edge and Arc. You choose what counts as studying.

🔔

Warn or force

Warn mode sends a notification once you've been off task too long. Force mode also switches you back to your study app or page.

🎭

Pick a personality

Base, Kind or Stern. Same reminders, very different tone. You can write your own messages too.

👀

Focus Mode

Optional. Uses your webcam to notice when you look away from the screen for too long. Off by default.

⏱️

Regular or Pomodoro

Count up, or set your own focus, break and round lengths. Pause freezes the timer too.

📈

Know where your time went

Productivity %, your last 7 days, per-class hours and your biggest distractions.

🗓️

Deadline-aware

Add midterms and finals. Tunnl flags the class you've neglected before something big is due.

How it works

Set up takes a couple of minutes.

Pick what counts

Click “Allow what I'm on now” and switch to each study app or site you use.

Start a session

Choose a class and a timer, then press Start. Tunnl logs your activity while you work.

Get nudged back

Drift off your list and you'll get a heads-up. Stop the session for a full summary.

Your data stays on your Mac

Everything runs locally. Sessions are stored in a small database on your computer, and the dashboard only listens on your own machine.

Focus Mode is opt-in. It uses Apple's on-device face detection. Video frames are never saved or sent anywhere, and the camera is off outside of sessions.
